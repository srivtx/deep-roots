How to read the map

Start anywhere: pick a module you care about, read its chapters, and follow its citations backward to the roots (they nearly all end at module 01, chapter 03 or chapter 05). Then follow them forward: the same page you just read is the prerequisite for three modules you have not opened yet. That is the whole design of this program: one root layer, twenty-two fields, and a thousand doors between them.
